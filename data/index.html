Free data sources

A working list of open data portals I use regularly, grouped by type. All are free; some require a no-cost account.

Last reviewed July 2026. If a link has broken, let me know.

Satellite imagery

Source What you get
USGS EarthExplorer Full Landsat archive (1972–present), ASTER, aerial photography
Copernicus Data Space Sentinel-1, -2, -3, -5P
NASA Earthdata Search MODIS, VIIRS, SMAP, GPM, ECOSTRESS
LP DAAC MODIS and VIIRS land products
HLS Harmonized Landsat + Sentinel-2, 30 m

Cloud platforms

Source What you get
Google Earth Engine Petabyte catalog, JavaScript and Python APIs
Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC catalog with hosted JupyterHub
AWS Open Data Landsat, Sentinel-2, NAIP in public S3 buckets
GEE Community Catalog Community datasets not in the official GEE catalog

Agriculture

Source What you get
CroplandCROS USDA Cropland Data Layer, 10 m from 2024
CDL bulk download National CDL rasters by year
USDA NASS Quick Stats County and state yield, acreage, production
AmeriFlux Eddy covariance flux tower measurements
USDA Ag Data Commons Data from USDA-funded research
FAOSTAT Global agricultural statistics by country

Weather and climate

Source What you get
Oklahoma Mesonet 120+ Oklahoma stations, 5-minute intervals
PRISM 4 km gridded US climate
Daymet 1 km daily weather, North America, 1980–
gridMET 4 km daily US meteorology, reference ET, drought indices
ERA5 Global hourly reanalysis, 1940–
NOAA Climate Data Online Station observations and normals

Administrative boundaries

Source What you get
geoBoundaries Global admin boundaries, open license
GADM Global admin areas (non-commercial use)
Natural Earth Public domain basemap vectors
US Census TIGER/Line US states, counties, tracts, roads, water
Geofabrik OpenStreetMap extracts by country

Terrain and soils

Source What you get
USGS 3DEP US elevation, 1 m lidar where available
Copernicus DEM Global 30 m DEM
SRTM Near-global 30 m elevation
Web Soil Survey US SSURGO soil properties
SoilGrids Global gridded soil properties, 250 m

Learning

Source What you get
NASA ARSET Free applied remote sensing training
GEE Developer Guide Official Earth Engine documentation
Spatial Thoughts Free QGIS, GEE, and Python course material

Suggestions welcome — email me if a useful source is missing.
