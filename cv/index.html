Professional Summary

I specialize in remote sensing and GIS applications in agriculture, applying machine learning and deep learning to geospatial analysis. My work integrates UAS and satellite imagery with in-situ and weather data to improve crop monitoring, yield prediction, and sustainable land management across agroecosystems.
